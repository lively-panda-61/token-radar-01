Every protocol below records no token yet. Each is priced the way a buyer prices a company with no public shares: from what the market already pays for the same kind of business. How the value is built, and what it is not

Implied value = TVL × category comps multiple × growth factor × maturity factor. Growth is the protocol's seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150% and weighted 0.40. Maturity is a hump in log-age peaking at 540 days: brand-new protocols are unproven, and a protocol that has run for years with no token may never issue one. The range beside each value is the same formula at the category's 25th and 75th percentile multiples.

The snapshot records 15 protocols with a market cap of zero and one with a negative market cap; both are missing records, not prices, and the comps table leaves them out. A protocol “records no token” in the snapshot — that is the record, not proof it has none. An implied value is this entry's own claim, never a price and never a prediction that a token will be issued, or when.
